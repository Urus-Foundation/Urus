# URUS Contributor Code of Conduct

The URUS project is committed to providing a friendly, professional,
and harassment-free environment for everyone — regardless of age,
background, body size, disability (visible or invisible), ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
with project-specific additions where they matter.

It applies to **everyone** participating in URUS spaces — code reviews,
issues, discussions, mailing lists, chat channels (when set up),
conference talks given under the URUS name, and any other forum where
your participation is connected to this project.

---

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, and to act and interact in ways that
contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

---

## Standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Being kind and patient toward newcomers.
- Demonstrating empathy.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning.
- Focusing on what is best for the community rather than self.
- Writing clearly so non-native English speakers can follow.

Behaviour that is **unacceptable** includes:

- Sexualised language, imagery, or attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (real name, address, employer,
  immigration status, etc.) without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, talks, or other events.
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above.
- Bullying maintainers into accepting changes (raised voice, deadlines,
  threats of public criticism, mass-tagging accounts).
- Using the project as a platform to attack other communities,
  ecosystems, or individuals.
- Posting AI-generated text purported as a unique human contribution
  to a discussion that requires consent or attribution.

---

## Project-specific additions

URUS is a single-maintainer pre-alpha project. A few specifics:

1. **Be patient with the founder.** Pre-alpha bandwidth is limited.
   A delayed response is not a rejection.
2. **Do not litigate governance in code review.** Disagreements with
   project direction belong in issues with the `governance` label, not
   in PR review threads about unrelated patches.
3. **Do not weaponise the security disclosure process.** Reporting an
   issue to embarrass a maintainer or extract concessions outside the
   policy violates this code.
4. **Do not impersonate maintainers** or claim authority you do not
   have. This includes domain squatting, social-handle squatting, and
   forged email.
5. **Respect commercial-discussion confidentiality.** If you are
   involved in a commercial conversation under
   [`COMMERCIAL.md`](./COMMERCIAL.md), treat its contents as confidential
   unless explicitly told otherwise.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project founder and any appointed maintainers are responsible for
clarifying and enforcing this code. They will take fair, proportionate
corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

- remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues,
  and other contributions that violate this code;
- communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate;
- temporarily or permanently ban any participant for behaviours they
  deem unprofessional or threatening.

---

## Scope

This code applies within all project spaces, and also when an individual
is officially representing the project in public spaces. Examples
include: using an official project email address, posting via an
official social-media account, or acting as an appointed representative
at an online or offline event.

It also applies to **conduct adjacent to project participation** when
that conduct affects the safety or trust of project members —
for example, doxxing a maintainer on an unrelated platform.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code, report
it to:

- **urusfoundation@gmail.com** (placeholder) — preferred.
- **Rasya Andrean — `rasyaandrean@outlook.co.id`** — backup, or if the
  conduct issue involves the conduct address itself.

When reporting, include if possible:

- Your name and contact (anonymous reports are accepted but harder to
  resolve).
- The names or handles of those involved.
- Links, screenshots, or quotations of the behaviour.
- Date, time, and location (channel, repo, event).
- Whether you have already taken any action, and any restraining
  considerations.

**All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.**

All maintainers are obligated to respect the **privacy and security of
the reporter** during and after any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Following the Contributor Covenant guidelines, project leaders will
follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences
for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact** — Use of inappropriate language or other behaviour
deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence** — A private, written warning from a project leader,
providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation
of why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact** — A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence** — A formal warning with consequences for continued
behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with those enforcing this code, for a specified period of
time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as
external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to
a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community Impact** — A serious violation of community standards,
including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence** — A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public
or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with those enforcing this code, is allowed during this period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community Impact** — Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence** — A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction
within the community.

---

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action against you was incorrect:

1. Reply to the enforcement notice explaining your view, calmly.
2. If the action is upheld and you still disagree, you may appeal once
   in writing to `urusfoundation@gmail.com`. The appeal will be reviewed
   by the founder (or, where the founder is the subject of the conduct
   complaint, by an appointed neutral party).
3. The appeal decision is final.
